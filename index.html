The Story

Where the resilience comes from

2016

I grew up in Saharanpur without much of a financial safety net. A failed board exam in Economics could have ended things early — there was no backup plan, only the need to keep going.

'17–18

The rejections piled up after that — JMI, AMU, a closed door at Amazon, a first attempt at moving to the UAE that went nowhere. Each one stung, but each one also pushed me toward the work I could actually build on: my first job in accounts, at Al-Safa Industries.

'19

At 23, I bet my own savings on a venture of my own — Renovation Martz. It didn't survive. I came out of it with debt, and something more useful than any of my later wins: a real, lived understanding of risk, cash flow, and what it actually costs to recover from a bad position.

"The same instincts that once tracked my own debts now protect other people's revenue."

'19–23

I rebuilt from there — self-funding my own MBA while working full-time, managing relationships for property buyers and investors at Magicbricks, contributing to a marketing campaign that ran in The Times of India, and later driving B2B growth for Alibaba.com's US market, from India.

2025

In 2025 I moved to Dubai and joined a UK-based FMCG group as a Credit Controller. Seven months later, I was promoted to Account Manager — now running a 500+ account portfolio across the UK and EU, remotely, from the UAE. The next stop hasn't been booked yet.
